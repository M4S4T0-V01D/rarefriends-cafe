What's inside

A little street, fully alive.

Everything here is built around the Friends in your wallet: their on-chain sprites, families and generations.

★

Your Friend runs it

Your selected Generations NFT is the manager, drawn from its canonical on-chain sprite. Its family sets a perk: Hoverers float faster, Colossi calm guests, and so on.

♙

Your Friends staff it

Every other Friend you own can be a waiter, chef or promoter. Gen 1 is the top tier and Gen 6 the lowest. Each level earns a point for speed, stamina or skill; chefs bustle between the stoves, sink and prep top; tired Friends need a break.

⇆

A world outside

A main road and a side road meeting at your corner, lined with neighbouring shops that Friends walk in and out of, and eight sceneries from a cottage garden to a night market. Drag to look around, zoom, and turn the whole view a quarter at a time.

▦

Make it yours

Café, seafood, pastry, burgers or noodles, in one of seven buildings, from a slim bistro to L- and U-shaped cafés. Forty kinds of furniture, from a little cactus to a koi pond and a mini carousel, each turning four ways; 12 wallpapers, 12 floors, ten expansions.

✦

A cosy, long climb

Five-minute days, 22 levels, nineteen dishes per shop (seven master dishes for levels 16–22), thirteen upgrade tracks (a second pass gets dishes out faster), manager skills, daily challenges and random events: a food critic, a celebrity Friend, a tour bus, rain, a lunch rush.

♫

Smooth jazz, cute sounds

Sixteen procedural tracks (swing, bossa, jazz waltz and lo-fi) playing eleven composed melodies, a coffee-ready chime, a door bell, and Friends who chirp when happy and sigh when tired.

⧉

Saves, shares

Every managing Friend runs its own shop, saved by token number. Each day draws a report card you can post to X with @RareFriendsNFT #RareFriends #RareFriendsCafe.

A day at the shop

Five minutes of service.

  1. Open up. Friends walk the street; some step in and sit down, alone or with friends. Check today's three challenges.
  2. Serve. Tap a guest to take the table's order, tap the counter when the bell rings, and your Friend delivers. Watch for the critic.
  3. Look after your staff. Tap a tired worker to send them to the break room for 15–30 seconds.
  4. Grow. Spend tips on dishes, upgrades, staff slots, décor, a new building and expansions, then post your day card.
